12. USER DATA DELETION INSTRUCTIONS

In accordance with Meta Platform rules, you have the right to request the deletion of your user data associated with the HWLA Academy Facebook application.

Option 1: Remove the App via Facebook Settings (Instant Revocation)

  1. Log in to your Facebook profile.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. On the left menu, select Apps and Websites.
  4. Locate HWLA Academy in the list.
  5. Click Remove to revoke all access permissions immediately.

Option 2: Direct Deletion Request

If you want all associated workflow logs or cached token identifiers purged from our servers, send an email to gegegeawts@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - HWLA Academy". All associated data will be removed within 48 business hours.
